Tavio Academy

Learn Tavio at your own pace.

Tavio Academy is where your team learns the platform, organized by role. A new CSR can start with the client file and endorsements, while your accounting lead goes straight to insurer statements and month-end. Short guides cover single tasks, and recorded sessions explain new features.

Tavio Academy is being built alongside the platform. The learning paths and courses below show what's planned. Course availability, formats and access will be confirmed before launch.

Learning paths by role.Learning paths by role.

Each path starts with the essentials for that role, then builds toward the work people do every day.

  • Customer service representatives

    The client file, endorsements, documents, abeyances and handling portal requests. Coming soon.

  • Producers

    Quoting new business, working renewals, remarketing and rounding out accounts with the full client picture. Coming soon.

  • Accounting and trust

    Invoicing, receipts, commissions, insurer statements, trust reconciliation and month-end. Coming soon.

  • Principals and managers

    Dashboards, retention and producer reporting, workload views and using data to run the brokerage. Coming soon.

  • Administrators

    Users, roles and permissions, branches, templates, reminder schedules and AI settings. Coming soon.

  • AI with Tavio Intelligence

    Summaries, document reading, drafting and Ask Tavio, with good habits for reviewing every output. Coming soon.

FAQ

Academy questions.Academy questions.

Who can use Tavio Academy?

Tavio Academy is planned for every user at a customer brokerage. Access details, including any content open to the public, will be confirmed at launch.

Is training included in onboarding?

Role-based training is planned as part of every onboarding project. What's included for your brokerage is set out in your agreement.

Can we track our team's progress?

Progress tracking for administrators is planned. We'll confirm the details when the Academy launches.

What if I need help right now?

Visit the support page for in-app help, email and phone options, or browse the resource library for practical articles and guides.
