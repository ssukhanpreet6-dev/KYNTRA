A renewal week with Tavio

A renewal week, step by step.A renewal week,step by step.

An illustrative week at a personal lines desk, built from the steps Tavio takes. Every client and insurer in it is fictional.

  1. Monday

    Renewals open at 120 days.

    Every policy expiring in four months gets a renewal file, with the expiring terms attached and an owner assigned.

    Tavio Renew

  2. Tuesday

    The files at risk rise to the top.

    Each renewal is scored from premium change, claims and service history, with the reasons behind every score shown beside it.

    Tavio Intelligence

  3. Wednesday

    One remarket, every market.

    Where an increase crosses your threshold, the risk goes back out from the details already on file, and the quotes come back side by side.

    Tavio Quote

  4. Thursday

    Letters drafted, then approved.

    Each renewal letter is drafted from the facts on file. The account manager edits and approves it, and nothing is sent before that.

    Tavio Renew

  5. Friday

    Reminders go out on schedule.

    Clients who haven't replied get a reminder by email or text, sent only after their consent is checked.

    Tavio Remind

Trust and security

Security, explained control by control.Security, explainedcontrol by control.

Your clients trust you with their homes, businesses and families. Tavio is designed to protect that information from the first record.

Visit the Trust CenterSecurity details

  • Encryption by design

    Encryption in transit and at rest, with sensitive fields encrypted again in the application.

  • Mandatory MFA

    Multi-factor sign-in is required on every account.

  • Role-based access

    Everyone sees and approves only what their role allows.

  • Tenant isolation

    Each brokerage's data is walled off at the database level.

  • Audit trail

    Significant actions, including AI activity, are recorded and tamper-evident.

  • Encrypted backups

    Backups are encrypted at rest, and recovery commitments are set out in your agreement.

  • Hosting region in writing

    The hosting region for client records, documents and backups is set out in your agreement.
