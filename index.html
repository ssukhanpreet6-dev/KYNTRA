Three steps. No upheaval.Three steps.No upheaval.

Changing systems shouldn't put your clients at risk. We do the heavy lifting, and you sign off at every stage.

  1. 01

    Bring your book.

    We import your clients, policies and documents from your current system or spreadsheets, then reconcile them with you before anything goes live.

  2. 02

    Connect your markets.

    Link your email and SMS, and each insurer as its connection comes online, so quotes and messages flow through one place, tested in your own settings.

  3. 03

    Let the routine run.

    Renewals queue up for your approval; reminders go out on the schedule you set, only where consent is on file. You get back to people.

Private by design. Built for Canada.Private by design.Built for Canada.

Your clients trust you with their homes, their businesses and their families. Kyntra is built to honour that.

  • Encrypted, everywhere.

    Encrypted in transit and at rest, with the most sensitive fields encrypted again inside the application.

  • Built for Canadian data residency.

    Kyntra is designed to keep client records, documents and backups in a Canadian data region.

  • Privacy first.

    We keep only what brokering needs. No SINs, no card numbers, no credit scores. Consent is checked before every message.

  • Access by role.

    Everyone sees only what their role allows. Multi-factor sign-in is required for every account, and significant actions are recorded in an append-only audit trail.
