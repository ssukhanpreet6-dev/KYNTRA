Connectivity, insurer by insurer

Tavio Connect is being built on CSIO standards, and connections are set up insurer by insurer as they become available. We confirm in writing which of your insurers can connect, and when, and we never list an insurer as connected before that is confirmed. Documents that arrive as PDFs are read by Tavio Intelligence and confirmed by your team.

The same details, typed again and again.The same details, typed again and again.

Follow one new home policy through a typical brokerage. The CSR takes the details on the phone and enters them in the management system, then types them again into an insurer portal to quote. When the policy is issued, the documents are downloaded, renamed and filed, and the premium and policy number are keyed back in. An endorsement a month later starts the cycle again.

Each step is a chance for a transposed postal code, a misspelled name or a deductible that doesn't match the declarations page. Most are caught. The ones that slip through tend to surface at the worst moment, during a claim, and turn into an errors and omissions conversation.

FAQ

Questions about insurer connectivity.Questions about insurer connectivity.

What are CSIO standards?

CSIO, the Centre for Study of Insurance Operations, maintains the data and document standards used by Canadian property and casualty insurers and brokers. Building on them means information moves in a consistent, structured format between your brokerage and the insurers that support it.

Which insurers will be connected?

Connections are set up insurer by insurer as they become available, and depend on what each insurer supports. We confirm in writing which of your insurers can connect, and when.

Will we still need insurer portals?

For some tasks, yes. Connectivity takes on routine portal work, such as downloading documents and rekeying applications, but some transactions may still happen in an insurer's own systems.

What about insurers that only send PDFs?

Their documents still land in the download inbox. Tavio Intelligence reads them, suggests the client and policy and extracts key details for a person on your team to confirm before anything is saved.
