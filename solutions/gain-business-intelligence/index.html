In many brokerages, last month's numbers arrive halfway through this month. Someone exports policies from one system and commissions from another, reconciles them in a spreadsheet and sends a PDF that is already out of date. Ask a follow-up question and the process starts again.

The questions that matter most rarely fit a standard report. Which insurer relationships deserve more attention? Where is retention slipping, and why? Which producers are rounding out accounts, and which only write new business? What does the loss ratio look like before next month's insurer review?

FAQ

Questions about reporting.Questions about reporting.

How current is the data?

Dashboards are built from live platform data: the same records your team works in every day. Policies, accounting and renewals share one client file, so the numbers line up.

Can producers see each other's numbers?

Only if you decide they should. What each person can see follows their role, so a producer might see their own book while principals see the whole brokerage.

Can we use our own BI tools?

Yes. Tavio Insights can export to your accounting or business intelligence tools when you need to go further than the built-in dashboards.

How reliable are AI answers and summaries?

Ask Tavio and the monthly summaries link to the records they came from, so you can check every figure. Treat them as a fast starting point. The underlying reports remain the source of truth.

Do we need perfect data first?

No. Reporting from one system makes gaps easier to spot, such as policies with no producer assigned, so data quality tends to improve as you use it. We check record counts with you during migration.
