Pricing approach

Pricing shaped around your brokerage.

Tavio is priced around how your brokerage works: the size of your team, the modules you choose and the help your move needs. We don't publish prices yet. Talk to our sales team and you'll get a written proposal with every fee set out.

How a proposal comes together.How a proposal comes together.

Every brokerage starts with Tavio Core, the client file at the centre of the platform. From there, you add the modules that match your work: renewals and reminders, accounting, quoting, the client portal, reporting and AI. Some brokerages switch on most of the platform at once. Others start with the modules where the team loses the most time and add more later.

Your proposal sets out the modules you've chosen, the onboarding and migration your move needs, training for each role and the support you'll receive. We'd rather understand your brokerage before quoting a number, so the first conversation is about how you work today.

FAQ

Pricing questions.Pricing questions.

Why don't you publish prices?

Brokerages differ in size, lines of business and the modules they need, and the scope of a migration depends on the system you're moving from. A written proposal built around your brokerage is more useful than a price list that doesn't fit.

Can we start small and add modules later?

Yes. The platform is modular. Tavio Core is the foundation, and other modules can be added when your team is ready. Your proposal explains how adding modules affects pricing.

Are migration and training included?

Onboarding, migration and training are scoped for each brokerage and set out in your proposal, so you can see what each part covers.

How are beta features offered?

We'll tell you which features are in beta during your demo, and your proposal will say how they're included. Product pages show each module's current status.

Is there a cost to see a demo?

No. A demo is a conversation to help you decide whether Tavio fits your brokerage.
