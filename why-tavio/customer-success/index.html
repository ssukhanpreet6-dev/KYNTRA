Our support model.Our support model.

Different questions need different people. Here is who you'll work with.

  • Onboarding team

    Plans and runs your migration, configuration and training, and stays with you through the first weeks after go-live.

  • Customer success

    Your ongoing contact for adoption, new modules and getting more from the platform, with regular check-ins.

  • Product support

    Handles day-to-day questions and issues, from a stuck renewal to a permissions change.

  • Tavio Academy

    Live, role-based training for new starters, refreshers for experienced staff and walkthroughs when new features ship.

  • Product updates

    Clear release notes explaining what changed, who it affects and whether anything needs your attention.

  • Your feedback

    Ideas and requests go to the product team, and we tell you what happens to them.

Tavio Academy: live training by role.Tavio Academy: live training by role.

Tavio Academy is how your team learns the platform: live sessions run by our team as part of onboarding and organized by role, so a new CSR starts with the client file and endorsements while an accounting lead goes straight to insurer statements and month-end.

After go-live, sessions are available when you add people, take on a new module or want a refresher, and administrators get their own session on users, permissions, templates and AI settings.

FAQ

Support questions.Support questions.

What are your support hours?

Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Pacific, by email at support@tavio.ca. Service levels are set out in your agreement.

How do we report an urgent issue?

Email support@tavio.ca with URGENT in the subject line for anything that stops your team working, such as Tavio being unavailable or a key workflow being blocked. Other requests can come in by email or through the support form.

Is training included?

Training is part of every onboarding project, and live Tavio Academy sessions stay open to your team after go-live. What's included for your brokerage is set out in your agreement.

Will we have a named contact?

Your customer success contact and how often you meet depend on your brokerage's size and plan. We'll confirm it during onboarding.
