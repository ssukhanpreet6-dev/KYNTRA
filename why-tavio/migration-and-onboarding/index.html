Migration and onboarding

Your book, moved with care.

Switching systems is mostly about trust in your data. We plan the move with you, run test migrations your team reviews, reconcile counts and balances, and train every role before anyone relies on Tavio for a live client.

How we approach a migration.How we approach a migration.

Every brokerage's data tells its own story: twenty years of clients, a few naming conventions that changed along the way, documents filed three different ways. A good migration respects that history and is honest about what can and can't come across cleanly.

So we start with discovery, agree exactly what moves and how it maps, and then run at least one test migration before the real one. Your team checks real files in Tavio, we reconcile the counts and the accounting balances together, and nothing goes live until you've signed off.

    Final migration and cut-over

What we'll ask of your team.What we'll ask of your team.

A smooth migration is a joint effort. Here is what helps most.

  • A project lead with the authority to make decisions and sign off
  • Access to data exports from your current system, arranged with its provider if needed
  • Time from a few experienced CSRs and producers to review test files
  • Your accounting lead for balance reconciliation
  • A go-live date that avoids month-end and your busiest renewal periods
  • A little clean-up before the move, such as merging known duplicate clients

FAQ

Migration questions.Migration questions.

Can we keep our old system during the move?

Yes. Your team keeps working in your current system until go-live. Whether you keep read-only access afterwards depends on your agreement with your current provider, so it's worth checking early.

Do we need to clean up our data first?

You don't need perfect data to start. We flag duplicates and gaps during mapping, and your team decides which ones are worth fixing before the move.

What about insurer connectivity and document downloads?

Insurer connectivity is set up for Tavio as part of onboarding. Availability varies by insurer and is confirmed during onboarding.

Will there be downtime?

The final migration is planned for a quiet period, such as a weekend, so your team can stop in one system and start in the other with as little disruption as possible.

How is our data protected during migration?

Migration data is handled under the same security controls described in the Trust Center, and access is limited to the people working on your project.
