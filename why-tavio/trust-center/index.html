  • Encryption

    Designed to encrypt connections with TLS and data at rest, with the details pending confirmation. Sensitive fields are encrypted again inside the application.

    Learn more

  • Role-based access

    Permissions decide who can see, change and approve what, so everyone sees only what their role allows.

    Row-level security in the database and application scoping on every request keep each brokerage's data walled off.

    Learn more

    Significant actions, including AI activity, are written to an append-only, tamper-evident audit trail.

  • Privacy by design

    We keep only what brokering needs. No social insurance numbers, card numbers or credit scores are stored.

Tavio is designed to keep client records, documents and backups in a Canadian data region. Some supporting services, such as email and text message delivery, may process message data outside Canada, and our subprocessor list shows where each provider processes data.

AI and your data.AI and your data.

AI features in Tavio work within the same protections as the rest of the platform. They read only what the person using them is allowed to see, every output is reviewed by a person before anything happens, and every AI action is recorded in the audit trail. Our AI approach page explains the principles in full, including our position on training AI models.

General information, not legal advice

Tavio provides tools that support your brokerage's privacy and regulatory obligations. Your brokerage remains responsible for meeting them. Speak with your privacy officer or legal adviser about your specific requirements.

FAQ

Trust questions.Trust questions.

Where is our data stored?

Tavio is designed to keep client records, documents and backups in a Canadian data region. The hosting region is pending confirmation, and our security page lists each subprocessor and where it processes data.

Do you have a SOC 2 report?

Not yet. A SOC 2 Type II audit and an independent penetration test are planned. We'll update this page when reports exist, and we don't display badges until then.

Can Tavio staff see our client data?

Access to customer data is limited and controlled. Details of how support access works will be published on our security page. [Support access policy]

Is our data used to train AI models?

Our intended principle is that your client data is not used to train AI models unless your brokerage decides otherwise. The final commitment is being confirmed. See our AI approach for details.

How do we report a security concern?

Use the responsible disclosure contact on our security page. Please don't access client data or disrupt the service while testing.
