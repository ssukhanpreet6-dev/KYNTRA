Built on industry standards.Built on industry standards.

Canadian brokerages and insurers exchange information using standards maintained by CSIO, the Centre for Study of Insurance Operations. Tavio is designed around those standards for insurer connectivity and electronic documents, so policy data and documents can move between insurers and the client file without being retyped.

Beyond insurers, Tavio is built to connect to everyday tools such as business email and calendars, e-signature, card and pre-authorized debit payments, telephony and accounting. For anything else, the Tavio API and webhooks let your systems read from and react to what happens in Tavio. The catalogue below describes each connection.

FAQ

Integration questions.Integration questions.

Which insurers are connected?

Insurer connectivity uses CSIO standards and is set up insurer by insurer as connections become available. We confirm in writing which of your insurers can connect, and when.

Do you support CSIO eDocs?

Tavio is designed to receive insurer documents electronically using CSIO standards and file them to the right client and policy. Connections are set up insurer by insurer as they become available, and confirmed in writing.

Does Tavio work with our accounting software?

Tavio Ledger handles broker accounting, and accounting export sends summarized entries to your general ledger. Supported formats are confirmed during onboarding.

Can we connect our own tools?

Yes. The Tavio API and webhooks are built for exactly this. Their scope and documentation are confirmed with your team during onboarding.

We build software for brokers. Can we partner with you?

We'd like to hear from you. Visit our partners page or write to hello@tavio.ca.
