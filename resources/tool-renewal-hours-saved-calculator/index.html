Your estimate from your own inputs. It is not a Tavio claim, benchmark or guarantee.

This calculator estimates the time your team spends preparing renewals today and the time it could spend with more of the routine work automated. It uses only the numbers you enter. It isn't a benchmark, and it makes no claim about results at your brokerage.

How it works

  • Renewals per month: the number of policies coming up for renewal in a typical month, from your own book.
  • Minutes per renewal today: your estimate of the hands-on time spent opening, reviewing, writing to the client and following up on one renewal.
  • Share of that work automated: your own assumption, as a percentage, for the steps that would run without you, such as preparing files and sending reminders.
  • Hourly cost of staff time (optional): a loaded hourly cost, to put the hours in dollars.

Hours freed each month = renewals × minutes per renewal ÷ 60 × the share you expect to be automated. Hours a year are the monthly figure × 12, and the value is the hours × your hourly cost. Change any input to see how the result moves.

Getting a realistic estimate

  • Time a handful of real renewals this month, from the first look to the client letter, instead of guessing.
  • Run personal and commercial lines separately, since commercial renewals usually take much longer.
  • Leave the review and the client conversation out of the share you expect to be automated. Those stay with your team.
  • Treat the result as a planning figure, and check it against your own records after any change.

An estimate, not a promise

The result depends entirely on the inputs and assumptions you enter. Tavio doesn't publish typical savings, and nothing on this page is a claim about what your brokerage will achieve.
