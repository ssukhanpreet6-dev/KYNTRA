A renewal in Tavio, start to finish

Follow one home renewal through Tavio: opened at the first checkpoint, compared with the expiring policy, explained in a drafted letter and approved by the account manager.

  • Renewals
  • Product tour

One renewal, followed from the day it opens to the day the client hears from you, with a person approving every step.

What you'll see

  • The renewal pipeline, sorted by checkpoint and by risk
  • Renewal terms compared with the expiring policy, with changes highlighted
  • A remarket opened in Tavio Quote when the increase crosses your threshold
  • A renewal letter drafted, edited and approved
  • Follow-up reminders checked against the client's consent
