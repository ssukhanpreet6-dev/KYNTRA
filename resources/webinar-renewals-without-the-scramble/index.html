Renewals without the scramble

A live session on running renewals 120 days out: sorting by risk, setting triggers, remarketing with a reason and measuring retention by policy count and premium.

  • Renewals
  • Retention
  • Events

Renewal season can run to a schedule. In this live session, we'll walk through a practical renewal rhythm for personal and commercial lines, and show how to move the work earlier so your team spends its time on the files that need it.

What we'll cover

  • Building a renewal list 90 to 120 days out, and sorting it into standard, watch and remarket
  • Triggers that move a renewal out of the standard process
  • Early client conversations that set expectations and collect what you need
  • Remarketing with a documented reason, and recording client decisions
  • Measuring retention by policy count and by premium, and learning from lost business
  • A short look at how Tavio Renew supports this rhythm

Who should attend

Producers, account managers, CSRs and principals at Canadian brokerages. No Tavio experience needed.
