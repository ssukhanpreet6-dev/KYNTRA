Company

We build for the people who keep promises.

Every policy a broker places is a promise to a family or a business. Tavio is a Canadian software company, proudly built in British Columbia, making the platform that helps brokerages keep those promises with less admin and more time for clients.

Our storyCareers

Give brokers back their time.Give brokers back their time.

That's our mission, and it comes from a simple observation. Brokers are advisers. Their value lies in knowing a client's home, vehicles and business well enough to put the right coverage in place, and in being there when a claim happens. Yet much of the working day goes to searching for files, retyping details into insurer portals and chasing follow-ups that live in someone's memory.

Tavio brings the client file, quoting, renewals, accounting, client service and AI together in one cloud platform designed around the way Canadian brokerages work: CSIO standards, provincial licensing, Canadian privacy law and CASL consent. The system carries more of the routine load, so people can spend their time on the conversations that matter.

Four promises of our own.Four promises of our own.

The standards we hold ourselves to, in the product and in every conversation with a brokerage.

  • Keep the promise

    Every policy is a promise to a family or a business. Our work is helping brokers keep it.

  • Brokers stay in charge

    Tavio prepares the work. Brokers make the decisions, and their approval comes first, including for everything AI drafts.

  • Private by default

    We collect only what the work needs, and protect it from the very first record.

  • Say only what's true

    If something is planned, we say planned. If a number isn't proven, we don't print it.
