Careers

Build software brokers can rely on every day.

Insurance brokers protect homes, families and businesses across Canada. The software under their work decides how much of the day goes to clients and how much goes to admin. We're a British Columbia team building that software, and we're looking for people who care about doing it well.

Why this work matters.Why this work matters.

When a family's home floods or a contractor needs a certificate of insurance before a job can start, their broker is the person who answers. Behind that answer sits a stack of systems that, in many brokerages, still means retyping, searching and chasing. Better software gives those brokers time back for clients.

The domain is deep. A single client file touches insurers, provincial regulators, trust accounting, privacy law and CSIO data standards. If you enjoy learning how an industry really works and turning that into software people rely on, there's a lot here to learn.

What you'd work on.What you'd work on.

One platform, many hard problems.

  • The client file

    Clients, households, policies, claims, abeyances and documents in a single record that many people work on at once.

  • Insurer connectivity

    Quoting, policy downloads and electronic documents built on CSIO standards, with exceptions handled clearly.

  • Accounting and trust

    Invoices, commissions, insurer statements and trust accounting, where every cent has to reconcile.

  • AI with people in charge

    Document reading, summaries, drafting and Ask Tavio, designed so a person reviews and approves every output.

  • Client experiences

    A client portal, online payments and reminders that make dealing with a broker easier on any device.

  • Security and reliability

    Multi-factor sign-in, role-based access, encryption, audit trails and a platform brokerages can count on during renewal season.

How we work.How we work.

  • Close to brokers

    Everyone learns the domain: how a renewal runs, how an insurer statement reconciles and why an abeyance matters. Ideas are tested against real brokerage tasks.

  • Honest by default

    We say what we know and what we don't, in the product, on this site and with each other.

  • Privacy and security are everyone's job

    The data we handle belongs to brokerages and their clients. Protecting it is part of every role.

  • Craft, with care

    Calm interfaces, clear writing and code we're proud to maintain. Small details matter when people use a system all day.

How hiring works.How hiring works.

A typical process. Steps can vary by role, and we'll tell you what to expect at the start.

  1. 1

    Introduction

    A conversation about you, the role and Tavio. Ask us anything.

  2. 2

    Practical conversation

    A discussion grounded in the actual work, such as a design problem, a technical scenario or a brokerage workflow.

  3. 3

    Meet the team

    Time with the people you'd work alongside, so both sides know what working together would feel like.

  4. 4

    Decision

    A clear answer, with feedback where we can give it.
