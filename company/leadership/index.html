Leadership

How we lead.

A brokerage platform holds client records, trust money and the promises brokers make every day. The people who lead Tavio answer for getting that right, and these are the principles they work by. The best way to meet them is on a demo.

What leadership answers for.What leadership answers for.

Clear ownership of the things brokerages rely on most.

  • Product and the broker experience

    Deciding what we build and in what order, with brokerage workflows, accuracy and day-to-day usability as the test of every release.

  • Security and privacy

    Protecting client and brokerage data, and explaining plainly how the platform is secured.

    Learn more

  • Onboarding and customer success

    Careful migrations, training by role and support that understands brokerage. If a brokerage struggles, leadership hears about it.

    Learn more

  • Honest communication

    Making sure what we say on this site and in every demo is true: clear answers about every module, no invented numbers and no borrowed credibility.

Principles we lead by.Principles we lead by.

  • Brokers stay in charge of decisions, including everything AI prepares.
  • Unbuilt features are never described as ready, and unproven numbers stay off the page.
  • Security and privacy are reviewed while features are being designed.
  • We confirm modules, insurer connectivity and timelines in writing.
  • Feedback from brokerages reaches the people who decide what we build.

How decisions get made.How decisions get made.

The same few steps, whether the decision is a new feature or a change to how we support brokerages.

  1. 1

    Start from the brokerage

    Product decisions begin with a real task, such as a renewal, an endorsement or a month-end close, and the people who do it.

  2. 2

    Weigh privacy and security early

    Every change that touches client data is reviewed for privacy and security while it's designed.

  3. 3

    Write down the trade-offs

    Choices are recorded with the reasons behind them, so anyone on the team can see why a decision was made.

  4. 4

    Close the loop with brokerages

    Feedback reaches the people who decide what we build, and we tell brokerages what happens to it.
