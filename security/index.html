Encryption

Encrypted, everywhere.

Encrypted in transit and at rest, with the most sensitive fields encrypted again inside the application.

  • In transit

    Connections to Kyntra are encrypted with TLS.

    PLACEHOLDER[TLS version]

  • At rest

    Databases, documents and backups are encrypted at rest.

    PLACEHOLDER[Algorithm and key management]

  • Field level

    Sensitive fields are encrypted again inside the application with envelope encryption, so a database copy alone does not reveal them.

Tenant isolation

Each brokerage, walled off.

Row-level security in the database enforces the boundary between brokerages on every query. The application scopes every request to your brokerage as a second, independent check.

  • Row-level security at the database
  • Application scoping on every request

Identity and access

Access by role.

Multi-factor sign-in is mandatory for every account. Role-based permissions decide who can see, change and approve what, so everyone sees only what their role allows.

  • Mandatory multi-factor sign-in
  • Role-based permissions

Audit trail

A record that can't be rewritten.

Significant actions are written to an append-only audit trail. It is tamper-evident, so any change after the fact can be detected.

  • Append-only
  • Tamper-evident

Privacy and consent

Private by default.

We keep only what brokering needs.

Never stored
No social insurance numbers, no card numbers and no credit scores.
Privacy law
Designed around PIPEDA and BC PIPA.
Consent
Messages are checked against the client's consent before they are sent, designed with CASL in mind.
Privacy requests
Tools to find, export and correct a client's records when they ask.
Retention and deletion
Records are kept for as long as they are needed, then deleted.
