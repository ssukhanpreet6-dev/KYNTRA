Questions, answered.

Does Kyntra handle personal auto or ICBC Autoplan?

Not today. Kyntra is built for home, property and commercial lines.

Where is our data stored?

Kyntra is built to keep client records, documents and backups in a Canadian data region. Our security page sets out the details, including email and text-message providers that may process message data outside Canada.

How does migration work?

We import your clients, policies and documents from your current system's exports. You review a test import and correct anything mapped wrongly before going live, and an import can be rolled back.

Does anything reach a client without our approval?

Renewal offers, premium changes and remarket requests wait in your approval queue until someone with the right role approves them. Routine reminders go out on the schedule you set, and only to clients whose consent is on file.
