AI use case · Renewal risk prediction

PLACEHOLDERBeta

See which renewals need you first.

Tavio looks across every upcoming renewal and flags the clients most likely to leave, with the reasons in plain language. Your account managers decide who to call and what to say, weeks before the renewal date instead of the week after.

Book a demoSee Tavio Renew

Lapses rarely come out of nowhere.Lapses rarely come out of nowhere.

When a client leaves at renewal, the signs were often on the file: a sharp premium increase, a deductible that went up, a claim that took longer than it should have, months without a conversation. The trouble is that nobody has time to read every file in the renewal list looking for them.

Renewal risk prediction does that reading for you. It turns the signals already in Tavio into a simple score for each renewal, so the right accounts get attention while there is still time to remarket, explain the change or simply pick up the phone.

Signals behind the score.Signals behind the score.

Scores draw on signals such as these. The reasons behind each score are always shown next to it.

SignalWhy it matters
Premium changeWhy it mattersA large increase at renewal is one of the most common reasons clients shop around
Coverage changesWhy it mattersA higher deductible, a new sublimit or narrower wording can surprise a client who isn't told
Claims experienceWhy it mattersAn open or recently closed claim, and how it was handled, shapes how a client feels at renewal
Time since last contactWhy it mattersClients who haven't heard from the brokerage in months are easier for others to reach
Service historyWhy it mattersUnresolved requests or repeated changes can point to a relationship under strain
Tenure and relationshipWhy it mattersNewer clients, or clients with a single policy, may have fewer reasons to stay

  1. 1

    Scores refresh as the file changes

    As renewal terms arrive, claims move and activities are logged, each renewal's score and reasons update.

  2. 2

    Your list, sorted by risk

    Account managers see upcoming renewals ordered by risk, filtered by line, producer or branch.

  3. 3

    Tavio suggests a next step

    For example, call the client to explain a deductible change, or remarket in Tavio Quote. Suggestions are never actioned automatically.

  4. 4

    You decide and act

    Accept the suggestion as a task, choose a different approach or dismiss it. Calls, letters and remarkets are all handled by your team.

  5. 5

    Results feed back

    Retained and lost renewals show up in Tavio Insights, so you can see whether early contact is making a difference.

A score you can explain.A score you can explain.

  • Reasons, always

    Every score lists the signals that drove it, such as "Premium up 18%, open claim", so the account manager knows what to talk about.

  • Your rules stay in charge

    Scores add to your renewal checkpoints and remarketing rules. They don't replace the process your brokerage has set.

  • Visible to the right people

    Scores follow role-based access, so producers and account managers see the renewals they're responsible for.

Beta

Renewal risk prediction is part of Tavio Intelligence, which is in beta. How scores are produced for your book is confirmed during your demo.

A prompt, never a verdict

A risk score is an estimate built from patterns in the file. It can't know about a client's new job or a conversation over coffee. Treat it as a prompt to look closer, and let the account manager's knowledge of the client make the call.

FAQ

Renewal risk questions.Renewal risk questions.

How far ahead are renewals scored?

Scores appear on upcoming renewals within the windows your brokerage sets for its renewal checkpoints, so they line up with the way your team already works.

Does it contact clients automatically?

No. It suggests next steps, and your team decides whether to call, write, remarket or do nothing. Anything sent to a client goes through review and consent checks first.

Is it accurate from day one?

Scores depend on the history available in Tavio. Brokerages with complete records of premiums, claims and client contact will see more useful scores, and the reasons shown help you judge each one.

Can we see risk across the whole book?

Yes. Renewal risk appears on the renewal pipeline and on dashboards in Tavio Insights, by producer, branch, line of business and insurer.
