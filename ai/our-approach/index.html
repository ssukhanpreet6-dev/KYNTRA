Our AI approach

Useful AI, with people in charge.

Your clients trust your brokerage with their homes, vehicles and businesses, and your licensed staff stand behind the advice they give. AI in Tavio is designed to make that work lighter without loosening your hold on it.

Why we take a careful line.Why we take a careful line.

A brokerage is a regulated business that runs on personal information and professional judgement. A wrong limit in a renewal letter, a coverage explanation that overstates what a policy does, or a message sent without consent all carry real consequences for the client, for the broker's licence and for your errors and omissions exposure.

So we start from a simple position. AI should take on the reading, retyping and first drafts that fill a broker's day, and it should make its work easy to check. The decisions that matter, from what goes to a client to what is saved on a policy, stay with your people.

Six principles behind every feature.Six principles behind every feature.

  • People decide

    Every extraction, draft, score and suggestion is presented for review. Nothing is sent, saved or changed until a person approves it.

  • Grounded in your records

    AI works from the client file, documents and history in Tavio, so answers reflect your brokerage's information.

  • Sources you can check

    Answers and summaries link to the records and document pages behind them, and uncertain values are flagged for a person to verify.

  • Permissions first

    AI can read only what the person using it is allowed to see, under the same role-based access and encryption as the rest of the platform.

  • Recorded and reviewable

    Every AI request and outcome is written to the audit trail: who asked, what was used and what the person did with the result.

  • You choose what's on

    Administrators decide which AI features are available and to which roles, so each brokerage adopts AI at its own pace.

What happens when someone uses AI in Tavio.What happens when someone uses AI in Tavio.

Take a CSR asking Tavio to read a renewal offer for Client 0142.

  1. 1

    A person starts it

    The CSR opens the renewal offer from the download inbox and asks Tavio to read it. Most AI features start this way. Background features, such as renewal risk scores, only ever produce suggestions.

  2. 2

    Tavio gathers what that person may see

    Only records within the CSR's permissions are used: the expiring policy, the client file and the new document.

  3. 3

    AI prepares a result with its sources

    Extracted coverages, limits and deductibles appear next to the page they came from, with differences from the expiring policy highlighted.

  4. 4

    The person reviews and decides

    The CSR accepts, corrects or dismisses each value. A field marked uncertain must be checked before it can be saved.

  5. 5

    The outcome is recorded

    The request, the sources used and the CSR's decisions are written to the audit trail, where a compliance officer can review them later.

Your data, handled as yours.Your data, handled as yours.

Client information used by AI features stays under the same protections as the rest of Tavio: encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access, tenant isolation between brokerages and an append-only audit trail. AI does not open a side door around those controls.

Tavio Intelligence uses your brokerage's information to serve your brokerage. One brokerage's records are never used to answer another brokerage's questions. The Trust Center and our security page explain how data is hosted, encrypted and protected.

AI training terms, in writing

Your brokerage's client data is used to serve your brokerage. Whether client data can be used to train AI models is set out in your agreement, along with the AI model providers Tavio uses and where they process data.

What AI in Tavio won't do.What AI in Tavio won't do.

Clear limits make AI easier to trust. These hold across every module.

  • Send an email, text message or letter to a client without a person approving it
  • Save extracted data to a policy or client file before someone has reviewed it
  • Bind, change or cancel coverage, or post a payment or journal entry
  • Show anyone records their role doesn't allow them to see
  • Make underwriting or claims decisions, which remain with insurers and your licensed staff
  • Hide where an answer came from

General information, not legal advice

Tavio is designed around Canadian privacy law, including PIPEDA and BC PIPA. Your brokerage remains accountable for the personal information it collects and for the advice it gives, including when your team uses AI tools. Speak with your privacy officer or legal adviser about your specific obligations.

FAQ

Questions about our AI approach.Questions about our AI approach.

Is our client data used to train AI models?

Whether client data can be used to train AI models is set out in your agreement, along with the AI model providers Tavio uses and where they process data. Ask us for those terms before you sign.

Can a client tell that AI helped write a message?

Drafts are reviewed, edited and sent by your team, from your brokerage, under your staff member's name. Your brokerage decides how it describes its use of AI tools to clients, and we can talk through practical approaches during onboarding.

How accurate is it?

Accuracy depends on the document, the question and the data on file. That is why every output shows its sources, uncertain values are flagged and a person reviews the result before anything happens.

Who can see what AI did on a file?

AI requests and outcomes are part of the audit trail. People with the right permissions, such as a compliance officer or principal, can review them alongside every other change to the file.

Can we turn AI off for some roles?

Yes. Administrators choose which AI features are switched on and for which roles, module by module.
