Is your brokerage ready for AI?

Ten questions about how your brokerage works today. In about five minutes you'll get a plain-language result and the next steps that make sense for where you are, whether that's tidying up client records or piloting your first AI workflow.

Each answer scores from 0 to 3. Find your score below to see your result.

0–9 Start with the foundations
AI is only as useful as the information it reads, and today your client information and processes are spread across people and places. That's common, and it's the right place to start: the work that gets you ready for AI also makes the brokerage easier to run right now.
10–17 Building the base
You have the essentials in place, with some gaps in how documents arrive, how workflows are written down or how consent is recorded. Closing those gaps will make your first AI workflow far more reliable and much easier for the team to trust.
18–24 Ready to pilot
Your data, documents and processes are in good shape, and you have a sense of who should review AI-prepared work. You're ready to run a focused pilot on one workflow and learn from it before rolling out further.
25–30 Ready to scale
You have strong foundations, clear accountability and a team that's ready. You can introduce AI across several workflows at once, with governance that keeps pace as adoption grows.

What the assessment looks at.What the assessment looks at.

AI readiness has less to do with technology than with the information and habits it depends on.

  • Your data

    Whether client and policy information lives in one place, and how complete and consistent it is.

  • Your documents

    How declarations pages, policies and renewal offers reach the client file, and how much is handled by hand.

  • Your processes

    How clearly new business, renewals and endorsements are written down, and who checks what.

  • Your team

    How people feel about AI, whether someone is ready to lead, and who would review AI-prepared work.

  • Consent

    How email and text message consent is recorded, so messages only go to clients who have agreed to them.

  • Privacy

    Who is accountable for privacy and whether staff have written guidance on using AI with client information.

How to read your result.How to read your result.

Your result places your brokerage in one of four stages: start with the foundations, building the base, ready to pilot, or ready to scale. Each stage comes with practical next steps and the Tavio products most relevant to them.

The result is qualitative. It isn't a benchmark against other brokerages, a score for your staff or an audit of your systems. It reflects your own answers, and it is most useful as the start of a conversation inside your brokerage about where AI could help first.

General guidance

The assessment offers general guidance based on your answers. It isn't legal, privacy or compliance advice. For questions about your obligations under privacy law or CASL, speak with your privacy officer or legal adviser.

FAQ

About the assessment.About the assessment.

Who should take it?

Whoever knows day-to-day operations best: often the principal or operations manager, ideally with input from a senior CSR. Answering together is a useful exercise in itself.

Does a low result mean we can't use AI?

No. It means a few foundations are worth putting in place first, such as one system of record and consistent consent records. Those steps make the brokerage easier to run whether or not you use AI.

What happens to my contact details?

If you choose to share them, we use them to follow up about your result, as set out in the consent text and our privacy policy. You can withdraw your consent at any time.

Can we retake it later?

Yes. Many of the next steps take a few weeks, so retaking the assessment after making changes is a simple way to see progress.
