Get the fastest answer.Get the fastest answer.

A few details up front save a lot of back and forth.

  1. 1

    Say what you were trying to do

    For example, "issuing an endorsement to add a vehicle to a commercial fleet schedule", and what happened instead.

  2. 2

    Reference records, not personal details

    Use the client number, policy number or invoice number. Please don't email sensitive client information such as driver's licence numbers or banking details.

  3. 3

    Include the screen and the time

    Note which screen you were on and roughly when it happened. A screenshot helps, with personal information hidden.

  4. 4

    Tell us the impact

    One user or the whole office? A single file, or every renewal due this week? Impact sets the priority.

FAQ

Common questions.Common questions.

I can't sign in. What should I do?

Use the sign-in link in your welcome email, then try resetting your password from the sign-in page. If you've lost access to your multi-factor device, your brokerage's Tavio administrator can help, or write to support@tavio.ca.

How do I add or remove a user?

Your brokerage's Tavio administrator manages users, roles and permissions. If you are the administrator and need help, send a support request.

A client can't access the client portal. Can you help?

Clients should contact their brokerage first, since the brokerage manages portal invitations. If your team needs help with a portal issue, send us a request with the client number.

How do I report a security concern?

Write to security@tavio.ca straight away, or send a support request marked URGENT. Don't forward suspicious messages to colleagues.

Where can I see what changed in the latest release?

Release notes are published in the resource library under product updates, with notes on who each change affects.
