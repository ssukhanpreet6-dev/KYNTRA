Tavio Remind

Every follow-up, sent on time.

Tavio Remind sends the routine messages your team means to send, from renewal notices to payment reminders, by email and SMS in your brokerage's name, and only with consent on file.

The challenge

Good intentions don't send reminders.Good intentions don't send reminders.

Every CSR keeps a list of clients to chase: the signed declination still missing, the premium seven days overdue, the renewal nobody has acknowledged. When the phones get busy, the list waits. Clients notice the silence, and the overdue balance grows.

  1. 01

    Chasing by hand

    Payment and document follow-ups depend on someone remembering, and they stop when that person is on holiday or off sick.

  2. 02

    The wrong channel

    Some clients answer texts and ignore email. Others want email only. Without preferences on file, messages miss the person they're for.

  3. 03

    Consent in a spreadsheet

    When consent and opt-outs live outside the client file, every send means checking a separate list by hand, and mistakes are easy to make.

Capabilities

Reminders that respect the client.Reminders that respect the client.

Set the schedule once. Tavio Remind sends each message at the right time, through the right channel, and stops the moment it's no longer needed.

  1. 01

    Payment reminders until it's paid

    Build a sequence for overdue premiums, such as day 7, 15 and 30, with a different message at each step. The sequence stops on its own as soon as the payment is recorded in Tavio Ledger or Tavio Pay.

  2. 02

    Renewal notices ahead of the date

    Tell clients a renewal is coming, what to check and how to reach you. Notices follow the renewal pipeline in Tavio Renew, so the timing tracks each policy and its checkpoints.

  3. 03

    Document requests that chase themselves

    Ask for a signed declination, a driver's licence or an updated vehicle list, with a secure upload link. Reminders continue until the document arrives, then stop and file it on the right policy.

  4. 04

    Consent checked before every send

    Each message is checked against the client's consent and contact preferences first. Opt-outs take effect right away, and SMS replies such as STOP are honoured automatically. Designed with CASL in mind.

  5. 05

    Quiet hours and time zones

    Messages scheduled inside your quiet hours wait for the morning, in the client's own time zone. A brokerage in Kelowna can serve clients in Calgary and Toronto, and each one hears from you at a reasonable hour.

  6. 06

    Birthdays and moments that matter

    Send a birthday note, a welcome after a new policy or a thank-you when a claim closes. Small touches, sent consistently, in your brokerage's voice and only where consent allows.

How it works

How Tavio Remind worksHow Tavio Remind works

  1. 1

    Pick a trigger

    Start from an event on the client file: a renewal checkpoint, an overdue invoice, a missing document or a date such as a birthday.

  2. 2

    Set the sequence

    Choose the channel, timing and wording for each step from your templates, and the condition that ends it, such as payment received.

  3. 3

    Let it run

    Tavio checks consent, respects quiet hours and logs every message on the client timeline, so your team can see what was sent and when.

FAQ

Questions, answered.Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Talk to our team.

How does Tavio Remind support CASL?

Each message is checked against the consent and contact preferences on the client file before it is sent, opt-outs take effect right away, and every send is logged on the client timeline. Tavio supports your CASL practices; your brokerage remains responsible for how consent is obtained and used. This is general information, not legal advice.

Do messages come from Tavio or from us?

From you. Messages go out in your brokerage's name. Email can be sent through your brokerage's connected business mailbox, and SMS uses a number set up for your brokerage.

Can we pause a reminder for one client?

Yes. Pause or end any sequence from the client's file, for example when a payment arrangement has been agreed over the phone.

What are quiet hours?

A window, set by your brokerage, when no messages are sent. Anything scheduled inside it waits until the window ends, in the client's local time zone.

Can clients pay or upload documents straight from a reminder?

Yes. Where Tavio Pay is enabled, payment reminders can include a secure payment link. Document requests include an upload link that files the document on the right policy.

Can we write our own messages?

Yes. Every message uses templates your brokerage controls, with merge fields for the client's name, policy, amount and due date. Tavio Intelligence can draft them for your team to review.
