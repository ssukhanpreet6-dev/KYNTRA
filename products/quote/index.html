Tavio Quote

One entry. Every market. Side by side.

Tavio Quote takes the risk details once and sends them to every insurer you're connected to. Premiums and conditions come back side by side, and each quote is saved to the file.

The challenge

Every insurer portal asks the same questions.Every insurer portal asks the same questions.

A new home prospect calls. Your CSR types the same address, roof year and claims history into three or four insurer portals, copies the premiums into a spreadsheet and hopes nothing was mistyped. By the time the comparison is ready, the prospect has called someone else.

  1. 01

    Hours lost to rekeying

    The same application is entered once per insurer, and every entry is another chance for a typo that changes the premium.

  2. 02

    Comparisons that don't line up

    Premiums arrive in different formats with different deductibles and conditions, so the spreadsheet ends up comparing numbers that don't match.

  3. 03

    Quotes that vanish

    Quotes the client didn't choose are rarely saved, so at renewal nobody knows what the market said last year.

Capabilities

Quoting built around one application.Quoting built around one application.

Tavio Quote covers the work from the first question to the bind request, in personal and commercial lines.

  1. 01

    One application for every insurer

    Capture the risk once in a guided application that asks only what the selected lines and insurers need. Tavio maps your answers to each insurer's requirements, so nothing is typed twice.

    • Home, tenant, condo and seasonal dwellings
    • Personal auto: on the roadmap, not available today
    • Small commercial packages, property and liability
  2. 02

    Results side by side

    Premiums, deductibles, limits, discounts and underwriting messages return in one comparison, laid out so you compare like with like. Sort by price or coverage and see at a glance where an insurer has declined or referred.

  3. 03

    Submissions for markets without online rating

    Some insurers and MGAs quote through an underwriter. Send them the same application as a clean submission package, track who has replied, and add their terms to the comparison when they arrive.

  4. 04

    Every quote kept on the file

    Chosen or not, each quote is saved to the client record with the answers it was based on. At renewal or during a remarket, you can see what every market offered last time and why the client chose what they did.

  5. 05

    From comparison to bind request

    Turn the chosen option into a client-ready proposal, collect an e-signature on the application and send the bind request to the insurer. The policy is created in Tavio Core with the quoted details already in place.

How it works

How Tavio Quote worksHow Tavio Quote works

  1. 1

    Start from what you know

    Begin a quote for a new prospect or an existing client. Details already on the file in Tavio Core fill the application for you.

  2. 2

    Choose the markets

    Pick the insurers for this risk, or use saved market lists by line and region. Connection to each insurer varies by insurer and is confirmed during onboarding.

  3. 3

    Compare and advise

    Review results side by side, note the coverage differences that matter and send the client a clear proposal.

  4. 4

    Bind and keep the record

    Send the bind request, create the policy and keep every quote on the file for the next renewal.

FAQ

Questions, answered.Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Talk to our team.

Which insurers can we quote through Tavio Quote?

It depends on the insurers you hold contracts with and the connections each one supports. Insurer availability varies by insurer and is confirmed during onboarding. Markets without online rating can still receive the same application as a submission.

Does Tavio Quote handle commercial lines?

Yes. Tavio Quote covers small commercial packages, property and liability alongside personal lines. For larger or more complex commercial risks, Tavio Commercial adds location and vehicle schedules and multi-market submissions.

Do we have to retype details that are already on the client file?

No. When you quote an existing client, Tavio Quote reads the details already in Tavio Core. When the quote is bound, the policy is created from the quote, so the information flows both ways.

Are quotes the client didn't choose saved?

Yes. Every quote is stored on the client record with the answers it was based on. That helps at renewal, during a remarket, and when you need to show which options you presented if a coverage question comes up later.

How does Tavio Quote handle replacement cost for homes?

Where a replacement cost data provider is connected, you can run a valuation from the application and use the result to set the dwelling limit. Tavio Valuation covers property valuation in more depth.
