See where your book is going.

Tavio Insights turns the data in Tavio into dashboards and reports. Premium, retention, new business and loss ratios come from the platform, and every number opens the clients behind it.

The challenge

The numbers arrive after the decisions.The numbers arrive after the decisions.

In many brokerages the numbers live in exported spreadsheets that one person knows how to build. By the time the report is ready, the renewals it describes have already been won or lost, and nobody is sure two reports agree.

  1. 01

    Reports built by hand

    Exports, pivot tables and copy-paste take hours each month, and the knowledge of how they fit together sits with one person.

  2. 02

    Late answers

    Retention and production problems show up weeks after the fact, when there is little left to do about them.

  3. 03

    Numbers without names

    A chart that says retention dipped doesn't say which clients left or why. Someone still has to go and dig.

Capabilities

Answers for every seat in the brokerage.Answers for every seat in the brokerage.

Principals see the whole business. Managers see their branch or team. Producers see their own book. Everyone works from the same numbers.

  1. 01

    Book of business at a glance

    Written premium, policy count and commission by line, insurer, branch and producer, with trends over time and comparisons to the same period last year.

  2. 02

    Retention you can act on

    Track policy and premium retention by line, producer and insurer, then open the list of lost and at-risk clients behind each figure.

    • Policy count and premium retention side by side
    • Reasons for lapses, cancellations and moves to another broker
    • Drill from any figure to the client list
  3. 03

    Producer performance

    New business, renewals retained, cross-sell and commission for each producer, measured the same way for everyone and visible to the people you choose.

  4. 04

    Loss-ratio views

    See loss ratios by insurer, line and client from the premium and claims recorded in Tavio, so you know where you stand before insurer reviews and profit-sharing conversations.

  5. 05

    Cross-sell and account rounding

    Find clients who hold one policy and could reasonably hold more, such as a home without an umbrella, or a business owner with no personal lines.

  6. 06

    Scheduled reports and exports

    Send a weekly renewal pipeline to managers or a monthly production report to principals, and export to your accounting or BI tools when you need to go further.

FAQ

Questions, answered.Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Talk to our team.

Where does the data in Tavio Insights come from?

From the platform itself: clients, policies, premium and commission from Tavio Core and Ledger, and renewals from Tavio Renew. Reports reflect what is recorded in Tavio, so clean data at migration matters, and our onboarding team helps you check it.

Can producers see each other's numbers?

Only if you allow it. Access follows each person's role, so a producer can be limited to their own book while principals see the whole brokerage.

How are loss ratios calculated?

From the premium and claims amounts recorded in Tavio, grouped by insurer, line or client. Insurers may calculate loss ratios differently, so treat Tavio's view as your working picture and check it against insurer reports.

Can we build our own reports?

Yes. Start from a ready-made dashboard, change the filters and groupings, and save the result as your own view. For deeper analysis, export the data to your BI tool.

Can we export data to a spreadsheet or BI tool?

Yes. Reports can be exported, and the BI export integration makes your data available to the analytics tools your team already uses.
