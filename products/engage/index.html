Tavio Engage

PLACEHOLDERBeta

Grow the book you already have.

Tavio Engage turns client data into campaigns that fit, from umbrella offers for homeowners to follow-ups for quoted prospects. Consent is checked on every send, and results land on the file.

The challenge

Your best prospects are already clients.Your best prospects are already clients.

A client who insures their home with you may have their cabin, their small business or their umbrella coverage somewhere else. The data to spot that is already in your system, but turning it into a campaign means exporting lists, cleaning spreadsheets and checking consent by hand, so it rarely happens.

  1. 01

    Lists that go stale

    An exported spreadsheet is out of date the day after it's pulled, and unsubscribes never make it back to the client file.

  2. 02

    Tools that don't know insurance

    General marketing tools don't know what a policy, a line of business or a renewal date is, so every segment is a workaround.

  3. 03

    Results that stop at clicks

    Opens and clicks are easy to count. Knowing which campaign led to a quote or a bound policy usually isn't.

Capabilities

Campaigns that understand insurance.Campaigns that understand insurance.

Tavio Engage works from the same client and policy data as the rest of the platform, so segments stay current and every result ties back to a client.

  1. 01

    Segments built from policy data

    Build audiences from lines held, insurers, renewal dates, location, tenure and consent. Segments update themselves as policies change, so a campaign reaches the people it was built for.

    • Lines held with you, and lines that aren't
    • Renewal month, branch and producer
    • Consent status applied automatically
  2. 02

    Cross-sell and up-sell journeys

    Build multi-step journeys that offer the next sensible coverage, such as umbrella liability for homeowners or business insurance for clients with a home-based business. Each step waits for a click, a reply or a set number of days.

  3. 03

    Newsletters without the busywork

    Send a regular newsletter to each audience: seasonal advice for homeowners, risk management notes for commercial clients. Start from brokerage templates and keep your brand on every send.

  4. 04

    Lead nurturing for prospects

    Prospects who asked for a quote but didn't bind get a thoughtful follow-up sequence, timed to the expiry date of their current policy. When they reply, the lead goes straight back to the producer.

  5. 05

    Consent built in

    Every campaign checks consent at send time. Express and implied consent are recorded with their source and date, implied consent is flagged before it lapses, and each email carries your brokerage's identification and an unsubscribe link.

  6. 06

    Analytics that reach the policy

    See opens, clicks and replies, then the quotes, bound policies and premium that followed each campaign. Compare campaigns by segment and keep the ones worth repeating.

How it works

    Build a segment from client and policy data, or start from a ready-made one, such as clients renewing next quarter.

FAQ

Questions, answered.Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Talk to our team.

Is Tavio Engage available now?

Tavio Engage is in beta. We'll show you the current feature set during your demo and talk through what is planned next.

How does Tavio Engage support CASL?

Consent is recorded on the client file with its type, source and date. Every send checks consent first, unsubscribes take effect right away, and campaign emails include your brokerage's identification and an unsubscribe link. Your brokerage remains responsible for how consent is obtained and used. This is general information, not legal advice.

How is Engage different from Tavio Remind?

Tavio Remind sends service messages tied to an event on the file, such as a payment due or a missing document. Tavio Engage runs marketing: campaigns, newsletters and journeys designed to grow the relationship.

Can we measure the premium that came from a campaign?

Yes. Quotes and bound policies that follow a campaign are linked back to it, so you can see premium written by campaign and by segment.

Can producers see what a client received?

Yes. Every message, click and reply appears on the client timeline in Tavio Core, so a producer knows what a client has seen before picking up the phone.
