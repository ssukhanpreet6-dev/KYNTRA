The challenge

The same details, typed onto form after form.The same details, typed onto form after form.

A client buys a vehicle, and someone copies their name, address, licence number, VIN and purchase price from a bill of sale and a registration onto a transfer form, a tax form and an insurance change. It's careful, repetitive work, and one transposed digit means doing it again.

  1. 01

    Retyping from paper

    Details are read off a registration or bill of sale and keyed in by hand, often more than once for the same transaction.

  2. 02

    Small errors, long delays

    A wrong VIN or a misspelled name can send a form back and leave the client waiting.

  3. 03

    Forms scattered across folders

    Blank forms live in shared drives and desk drawers, and nobody is quite sure which version is current.

Capabilities

What Tavio Forms is being built to doWhat Tavio Forms is being built to do

These features are planned for release and may change as development continues.

  1. 01

    Read the documents clients bring

    Upload a registration, driver's licence or bill of sale and Tavio will read the details and match them to the right client and vehicle.

  2. 02

    Vehicle transfer and tax forms, prefilled

    Where your brokerage handles vehicle registration, transfer and tax forms will be prefilled with buyer, seller and vehicle details, ready for your team to check. The transaction itself is still completed in the public insurer's systems.

  3. 03

    Insurance forms from the client file

    Applications, change requests and authorizations will draw on details already in Tavio Core, so the client's information is entered once.

  4. 04

    Review before anything is signed

    Each form will show which fields were filled automatically, which were typed and which are still missing, so checking a form takes a minute and nothing is retyped.

  5. 05

    Signed and filed

    Send forms for e-signature and the signed copy will be filed to the client and policy, with a record of who prepared and approved it.

How it works

How Tavio Forms will workHow Tavio Forms will work

  1. 1

    Collect the documents

    Clients upload documents through Tavio Portal, or your team scans them at the desk.

  2. 2

    Tavio fills the form

    Details are read from the documents and the client file, then placed in the right fields of the right form.

  3. 3

    Your team checks

    A CSR reviews the highlighted fields, completes anything missing and approves the form.

  4. 4

    Sign and file

    The form goes out for signature and the signed copy lands on the client file.

FAQ

Questions, answered.Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Talk to our team.

When will Tavio Forms be available?

Tavio Forms is coming soon. We haven't announced a release date, and the features described on this page may change before launch. Ask during your demo for the latest plan.

Which forms will be included at launch?

We'll confirm the launch form library before release. Vehicle transfer and tax forms are a focus for brokerages that handle vehicle registration, alongside applications, change requests and authorizations. Tell us which forms take your team the most time.

Will Tavio complete the vehicle transaction itself?

No. Tavio Forms is being built to prepare the paperwork for your team to check. Basic auto transactions are still completed in the public insurer's own systems, and Tavio does not handle personal auto policies today.

Will forms be submitted automatically?

No. Every form is reviewed and approved by a person on your team before it's signed or submitted.

What happens to the documents clients upload?

They're stored on the client file in Tavio with the same protections and audit trail as the rest of the platform. See the Trust Center for details.
