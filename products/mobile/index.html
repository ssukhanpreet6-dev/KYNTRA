Tavio Mobile

Coming soon

The client file, wherever the meeting is.

Tavio Mobile is the app we're building for producers who work away from the desk: client details, tasks and documents on your phone, with everything you capture sent back to Tavio Core.

Book a demoSee all products

Details may change before release.

The challenge

Good conversations happen away from the desk.Good conversations happen away from the desk.

Producers meet clients at the shop, on the farm or over coffee. Notes go into a notebook, photos sit in a personal camera roll, and the client file catches up days later, if it catches up at all.

  1. 01

    Notes typed up later

    Details from a site visit get written down twice, and the ones that matter most are often the ones that never make it into the file.

  2. 02

    Documents on personal phones

    Photos of a licence or a vehicle registration end up in a camera roll, outside your brokerage's controls.

  3. 03

    No context before the meeting

    Without the file to hand, a producer can walk into a renewal meeting without knowing a claim was opened last week.

Capabilities

What Tavio Mobile is being built to doWhat Tavio Mobile is being built to do

These are the features planned for release. We'll confirm the details as the app gets closer to launch.

  1. 01

    Client lookup in seconds

    Search any client you have access to and see their policies, open claims, recent activity and outstanding tasks before you walk in the door.

  2. 02

    Notes and calls, logged as you go

    Log a call, meeting or site visit from your phone. Dictate your notes and they'll be added to the client's activity timeline in Tavio Core.

  3. 03

    Capture documents with the camera

    Photograph a driver's licence, a vehicle registration or a property and file it to the right client and policy, without it sitting in your personal photos.

  4. 04

    Your day, in order

    See today's appointments, tasks and abeyances in one list, and get notified when something on your book needs attention.

  5. 05

    Ask Tavio on the move

    Ask a quick question about a client in plain language, such as when their home policy renews or whether a claim is still open.

How it works

How Tavio Mobile will workHow Tavio Mobile will work

  1. 1

    Sign in with your Tavio account

    The app is designed to use the same account, role and permissions as the web platform, so you see what you'd see at your desk and nothing more.

  2. 2

    Work from the same records

    The app will read and write the same client records as the web platform, so the office sees your updates as soon as you save them.

  3. 3

    Capture once

    Notes, photos and documents added on the phone will appear on the client file for the whole team.

AI inside Tavio Mobile

Tavio Intelligence is planned for the app from the start, to take the typing out of mobile work.

Explore Tavio Intelligence

  • Meeting summaries

    Dictate a few thoughts after a client visit and get a structured note: what was discussed, coverage questions raised and next steps.

    You stay in control. You review and edit the summary before it's saved to the client file.

  • Document reading

    Photograph a registration or a declarations page and Tavio suggests the details to update, such as the VIN, vehicle description or policy dates.

FAQ

Questions, answered.Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Talk to our team.

When will Tavio Mobile be available?

Tavio Mobile is coming soon. We haven't announced a release date, and the features described on this page may change before launch. Ask during your demo and we'll share the latest plan.

Which phones will it support?

We'll confirm supported devices and operating system versions before release.

Will it use the same permissions as the web platform?

That's the plan. The app is designed to use your existing Tavio account, role and permissions, so a producer sees their own book and nothing more.

Can our team work away from the office without the app?

Tavio runs in the browser, so the modules your brokerage uses can be reached from the office or from home on a laptop. Tavio Mobile will add features built for the phone, such as camera capture and dictated notes.

What will happen to photos taken in the app?

Documents captured in the app are planned to go straight to the client file in Tavio, with the same protections and audit trail as the rest of the platform.
