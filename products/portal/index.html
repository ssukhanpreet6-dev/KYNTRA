Tavio Portal

Open for your clients, even when you're closed.

Tavio Portal gives every client a secure place to see policies, download documents, ask for changes and pay, at any hour, with pink cards coming soon. Every request lands on the client file.

The challenge

Most service calls are simple. They still take a person.Most service calls are simple. They still take a person.

A business client needs a pink card for a new delivery van. Another wants a mortgagee letter for a refinance, or last year's declarations page for the bank. Someone else wants to know when their next payment comes out. Each request takes a minute on its own. Together they fill the phone queue and pull CSRs away from work that needs a licensed broker.

  1. 01

    After-hours requests wait

    Clients who buy a car on Saturday or close on a house in the evening can't reach the office, so they wait until Monday or call around.

  2. 02

    Documents travel by email

    Pink cards, declarations pages, lender letters and receipts get attached, forwarded and sent again, with no record of what the client already has.

  3. 03

    Changes arrive in pieces

    A new lender comes in by voicemail, the loan number by text and the closing date by email. Someone has to put the request together before anyone can act on it.

Capabilities

Self-service that stays connected to the file.Self-service that stays connected to the file.

Clients get answers on their own schedule. Your team gets complete, structured requests it can act on straight away.

  1. 01

    Policies at a glance

    Clients see every policy they hold with your brokerage, with the insurer, policy number, term dates and a plain-language summary of what each one covers.

    • Home, condo, tenant and business policies in one view
    • Renewal dates and offers clearly marked
    • Your brokerage's contact details on every page
  2. 02

    Documents, on demand

    Clients download declarations pages, receipts and lender letters as soon as your team shares them. Each download is recorded, so you always know what the client has in hand.

    • Declarations pages and renewal offers
    • Receipts, lender letters and broker letters
    • You choose which documents each client can see
  3. 03Coming soon

    Pink cards, on demand

    Business clients will download pink cards (proof of auto insurance) for the commercial vehicles on their policies as soon as your team shares them, where your insurers provide electronic proof of insurance. Availability varies by province and insurer.

    • A pink card for each vehicle on the fleet schedule, ready to print or show
    • Your team decides which cards to share
    • Every download recorded on the client file
  4. 04

    Change requests your team can act on

    Guided forms ask for what each change needs, from a new address to a lender's mortgage clause. Requests arrive in Tavio Core as tasks with every detail attached.

    • Change of address, lender or mortgagee
    • Coverage questions and new items to insure
    • Clients follow the status of each request
  5. 05

    Pay online

    Clients pay broker-billed invoices by card or pre-authorized debit through Tavio Pay, see what's coming due and download receipts. Payments post to Tavio Ledger, so accounting sees them without rekeying.

  6. 06

    Access for the whole household or business

    Invite more than one person to an account and decide what each can see, from a spouse on every policy to a bookkeeper who only needs invoices.

How it works

How Tavio Portal worksHow Tavio Portal works

  1. 1

    Invite your clients

    Send portal invitations from the client file, one at a time or as part of your renewal process.

  2. 2

    Choose what they see

    Decide which policies, documents and requests are available, for the whole brokerage or for a single client.

  3. 3

    Requests reach your team

    Change requests, uploads and payments land on the client file in Tavio Core, assigned to the right person.

  4. 4

    Your team confirms

    A CSR reviews each request, processes it with the insurer and confirms it to the client, who sees the status update in the portal.

FAQ

Questions, answered.Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Talk to our team.

Does a change request in the portal change the client's coverage?

Not on its own. Each request goes to your team as a task. Coverage changes once your brokerage has processed the request with the insurer and confirmed it to the client. The portal shows the status of each request along the way.

Can clients download pink cards and other documents?

Documents, yes. When your team shares a declarations page, a receipt or a lender letter, the client can download or print it. Pink card downloads for commercial vehicles are coming soon, where your insurers provide electronic proof of insurance; availability varies by province and insurer. The documents available always depend on what each insurer issues.

Do clients need to install an app?

No. The portal runs in a modern web browser on a phone, tablet or computer.

How do online payments work?

Clients pay broker-billed invoices by card or pre-authorized debit through Tavio Pay, and payments post to Tavio Ledger. Policies billed directly by the insurer continue to be paid to the insurer. Available payment options are confirmed during onboarding.

Can more than one person share an account?

Yes. A household or business can have several logins, and your team controls which policies, documents and actions each person can see.

How is the information clients enter protected?

It's stored on the client file in Tavio Core with the same protections as the rest of the platform, and requests, uploads and downloads are recorded in the audit trail. See the Trust Center for details on security and privacy.
