Tavio Commercial

Commercial accounts, built once and kept current.

Tavio Commercial brings applications, schedules and submissions into one workflow. Capture the account once, keep locations, vehicles and equipment in structured schedules, and send a complete package to each market.

Tavio is rolling out module by module. Your proposal confirms which modules are live for your go-live date. Supported lines and insurer connectivity are confirmed during onboarding.

The challenge

Every submission starts with a blank application.Every submission starts with a blank application.

Commercial accounts carry the most detail and the most rekeying. The same revenue, payroll, locations and loss history get typed into a different application for each market, fleet lists live in spreadsheets, and market responses are tracked in someone's inbox.

  1. 01

    Rekeying for every market

    Each insurer's application asks the same questions in a different order, so the account gets typed again for every submission.

  2. 02

    Schedules in spreadsheets

    Location, fleet and equipment lists sit in separate files that drift out of date between renewals and mid-term changes.

  3. 03

    Responses scattered in email

    Quotes, declines and requests for more information arrive by email, and the status of a submission is whatever the producer remembers.

Capabilities

The commercial workflow, start to finish.The commercial workflow, start to finish.

Designed around how commercial brokers place business: gather the risk, package it well, market it, compare terms and keep it current.

  1. 01

    One application, reused

    Capture business details, operations, revenue, payroll and prior insurance once. Tavio carries them into each submission, using CSIO data standards where insurers support them.

  2. 02

    Location schedules

    Record each location with its address, construction, occupancy, protection and exposure details and values, in the structure underwriters expect.

    • Construction, occupancy, protection and exposure (COPE)
    • Building, contents and equipment values per location
    • Mortgagees and loss payees by location
  3. 03

    Vehicle and equipment schedules

    Keep fleet and equipment lists with VINs, serial numbers, values and garaging locations. Add a unit mid-term and the change carries through to the endorsement request.

  4. 04

    Submissions to several markets

    Choose the markets for an account, send each one a complete package and track every response in one place: quoted, declined or more information needed.

  5. 05

    A complete package, every time

    A checklist on each submission confirms the application, schedules, loss history and supporting documents are attached before anything goes to an underwriter.

  6. 06

    From quote to next renewal

    Compare quoted premiums, limits, deductibles and key conditions, record the client's decision, and carry this year's submission forward as the starting point for next year.

FAQ

Questions, answered.Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Talk to our team.

Which commercial lines does Tavio Commercial support?

It's designed for the small and mid-sized commercial accounts most brokerages write, such as commercial packages, property, liability and commercial auto. Supported lines and insurers are confirmed during onboarding.

Can we send submissions to insurers electronically?

Where an insurer supports CSIO-standards submissions and its connection is in place, Tavio is built to send them electronically. For other markets, Tavio produces a complete package to send by email or through the insurer's own portal. Connections are set up insurer by insurer as they become available, and confirmed in writing.

Can we import our existing schedules?

Yes. Location, vehicle and equipment schedules can be imported from spreadsheets, and Tavio Intelligence can read them from documents such as an expiring policy or a statement of values.

How are mid-term changes handled?

Add or remove a location, vehicle or piece of equipment and Tavio updates the schedule, creates the endorsement request and keeps the history, so you can see the schedule as it stood on any date.

Can we issue certificates of insurance?

Yes. Certificates are generated from the policy on file, so the details match what's in force. Certificate holders are saved for reuse, and every certificate issued is logged against the policy.
