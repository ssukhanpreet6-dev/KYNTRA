The books, balanced to the policy.

Tavio Ledger is broker accounting inside the platform. Every bind, endorsement and cancellation posts the right entries, so invoices, commissions, insurer payables and trust balances stay in step.

The challenge

Month-end shouldn't take a week.Month-end shouldn't take a week.

When accounting sits apart from the policy system, every endorsement is keyed twice, insurer statements are ticked off line by line, and the trust reconciliation waits until someone has a free afternoon. Small differences turn into large ones, and month-end runs late.

  1. 01

    Every change keyed twice

    Policy changes are entered in the broker system and again in accounting, and the two slowly drift apart.

  2. 02

    Statements ticked by hand

    Insurer statements are reconciled line by line against spreadsheets, and unexplained differences carry over month after month.

  3. 03

    Trust balances seen too late

    Trust is checked after the fact, at month-end, when it should be visible every day.

Capabilities

Accounting that knows what a policy is.Accounting that knows what a policy is.

Built for broker-billed and direct-billed business, with trust and operating funds kept clearly apart.

  1. 01

    Invoices from the transaction

    Binding, endorsing or cancelling a broker-billed policy creates the client invoice or credit for you, with applicable taxes and broker fees, and the matching amount owed to the insurer.

  2. 02

    Receivables you can act on

    See what every client owes, aged by 30, 60 and 90 days, with the policy behind each balance. Overdue items can start a Tavio Remind sequence, and payments taken through Tavio Pay post themselves.

  3. 03

    Commissions, tracked to the producer

    Commission is calculated on each transaction, followed from expected to received, and split between producers by the rules you set, including different rates for new and renewal business.

  4. 04

    Insurer statements reconciled

    Import or enter each insurer's statement, and Tavio matches its lines to your policy transactions. Matched items clear, differences are listed with the likely reason, and the remittance is calculated net of commission.

  5. 05

    Trust accounting, visible every day

    Premiums received in trust are tracked separately from operating funds, with balances owed to each insurer, earned commission ready to transfer, and a monthly trust reconciliation your principal can review and sign off.

    • Trust and operating funds kept apart
    • Balances owed by insurer
    • Monthly reconciliation report with sign-off
  6. 06

    Client statements and a calmer month-end

    Send clients clear statements of account, close each month with a shared checklist, and export to your general ledger or your accountant in the format they work with.

How it works

  1. 1

    Set up your books

    We configure your chart of accounts, bank and trust accounts, insurers and commission rules, and bring opening balances across from your current system.

  2. 2

    Policies post themselves

    Every policy transaction in Tavio Core creates its accounting entries, so nobody keys a change twice.

  3. 3

    Reconcile as you go

    Match payments, insurer statements and bank activity daily, instead of saving it all for the last week of the month.

  4. 4

    Close and report

    Work through the month-end checklist, review trust and commission reports, and export to your general ledger.

AI inside Tavio Ledger

AI that does the matchingAI that does the matching

Tavio Intelligence takes on the repetitive part of reconciliation and surfaces the numbers that deserve a second look.

Explore Tavio Intelligence

  • Suggested matches

    For statement lines and payments that don't match exactly, Tavio suggests the most likely transaction, such as a payment whose reference names the client but not the invoice.

    You stay in control. Suggested matches are never posted on their own. Someone with accounting permissions approves each one.

  • Anomaly flags

    Flags entries that look out of pattern, such as a commission rate that differs from the agreed rate for that insurer and line, or a billed premium that doesn't match the policy.

    You stay in control. Each flag explains why it was raised, and your team decides whether to correct, accept or dismiss it.

  • Ask Tavio about the numbers

    Ask plain-language questions, such as which insurers have unreconciled items older than 60 days or which clients owe more than $500.

    You stay in control. Answers respect accounting permissions and link to the entries behind them.

FAQ

Questions, answered.Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Talk to our team.

Does Tavio Ledger handle trust accounting?

Yes. Premiums held in trust are tracked separately from operating funds, with balances by insurer and a monthly trust reconciliation. Trust accounting requirements vary by province. Tavio supports your process, and your brokerage remains responsible for meeting your regulator's requirements. This is general information, not legal advice.

Does it support both broker-billed and direct-billed business?

Yes. Broker-billed policies create client invoices and amounts owed to insurers. Direct-billed policies track the commission you expect from each insurer, so both appear in one commission view.

How are producer commissions calculated?

You set split rules by producer, line of business, insurer, and new or renewal business. Tavio calculates the split on each transaction and produces a commission statement for review before anything is paid.

Can we keep our current general ledger?

You can. Tavio Ledger can export entries to your general ledger, or you can run broker accounting fully in Tavio. We'll work out the right setup with you during onboarding.

Does Ledger work for brokerages with several branches?

Yes. Each branch can keep its own bank and trust accounts and reports, with a consolidated view for principals and the accounting team.

Can our external accountant have access?

Yes. Give your accountant a role with read-only access to the reports they need. Role-based access controls what each person can see, and every action is recorded in the audit trail.
