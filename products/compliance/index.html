Audit-ready records, kept as you work.

The challenge

When the regulator calls, the evidence should already exist.When the regulator calls, the evidence should already exist.

Brokerages answer for who did what on every file, which clients agreed to which messages, and whether each licensee was licensed on the day they acted. In many offices that evidence is spread across spreadsheets, inboxes and memory, and pulling it together for a review takes days.

  1. 01

    Gaps in the trail

    When systems don't record changes in detail, it's hard to show who changed a policy, when, and what it said before.

  2. 02

    Consent on scraps

    Marketing consent kept in a notes field, or not recorded at all, makes it hard to show you had permission to send commercial messages under CASL.

  3. 03

    Licence dates in a spreadsheet

    Licence renewals, continuing education and E&O details tracked by hand are easy to miss until they're overdue.

Capabilities

Records and controls, built into daily work.Records and controls, built into daily work.

Tavio Compliance runs underneath every module, so the record is created as the work happens.

  1. 01

    A complete audit trail

    Changes, exports and access to client records are recorded with who did it, when and what changed, including the value before and after.

  2. 02

    Consent tracking

    Record express and implied consent for commercial electronic messages with the date, source and wording, and see when implied consent is about to lapse.

    • Express and implied consent, with source and date
    • Unsubscribe requests honoured across Tavio messaging
    • Privacy consent for collecting and using personal information
  3. 03

    Licensing records

    Keep each person's licences, provinces, licence levels, expiry dates, continuing education and E&O details in one place, with reminders well before anything lapses.

  4. 04

    Privacy requests and incidents

    Log access and correction requests and track them to completion. Record privacy incidents and the steps taken, in one register your privacy officer can review.

  5. 05

    Role-based access

    Permissions decide who can see and change what, down to sensitive fields and accounting, and every change to a permission is recorded too.

  6. 06

    Reports for reviews and audits

    Produce audit trail extracts, consent reports, licensing summaries and file-completeness reports for internal reviews, insurer audits and regulator requests.

FAQ

Questions, answered.Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Talk to our team.

Does Tavio Compliance meet our regulatory obligations for us?

Your obligations stay with your brokerage and its licensees. Tavio Compliance gives you the records, controls and reports that support them. Requirements vary by province and regulator. This is general information, not legal advice.

How does it help with CASL?

You can record express and implied consent with its source and date, see when implied consent will lapse and honour unsubscribe requests across Tavio messaging. How CASL applies to your messages is for your brokerage to decide, with legal advice where needed.

Can we track licensing across provinces?

Yes. Record each person's licences by province, with expiry dates, continuing education and E&O details, and get reminders before renewals are due. Regulators such as the Insurance Council of British Columbia, the Alberta Insurance Council and the Registered Insurance Brokers of Ontario (RIBO) each set their own requirements.

How does it help with privacy laws?

Tavio helps you record privacy consent, handle access and correction requests and log privacy incidents. Depending on where you operate, PIPEDA, BC PIPA, Alberta PIPA or Quebec Law 25 may apply. This is general information, not legal advice.

Is trust accounting part of Tavio Compliance?

Trust accounting is handled in Tavio Ledger, which keeps its own reconciliation history. Compliance reports can include Ledger's audit trail alongside the rest of the platform.
