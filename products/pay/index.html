Tavio Pay

Get paid without the paper chase.

Tavio Pay sends clients a secure link to pay by card or pre-authorized debit. Every payment is matched to its invoice and posted to Tavio Ledger the moment it lands.

Book a demoSee all products

Tavio is rolling out module by module. Your proposal confirms which modules are live for your go-live date. Payment methods, providers and fees are confirmed during onboarding.

The challenge

Collecting premiums still means cheques and phone calls.Collecting premiums still means cheques and phone calls.

Clients want to pay their broker the way they pay everything else. Instead, they mail a cheque, read a card number over the phone or drop by the office. Your team writes down the details, keys the payment and then works out which invoice it belongs to.

  1. 01

    Slow to collect

    Every payment that waits for a cheque or a callback arrives late, and some never arrive before the policy is at risk.

  2. 02

    Card details in the wrong places

    Card numbers written on sticky notes or typed into emails are a risk no brokerage wants to carry.

  3. 03

    Payments without a home

    A deposit with a vague reference can take an hour to trace back to the right client, policy and invoice.

Capabilities

Payments that land in the right place.Payments that land in the right place.

Tavio Pay covers the whole loop: request, pay, match and post.

  1. 01

    Payment links by email and SMS

    Send a secure payment link for any invoice, down payment or broker fee. Clients pay from their phone without creating an account, and your team sees when the link is opened and paid.

  2. 02

    Card and pre-authorized debit

    Accept credit cards for one-time payments and pre-authorized debit for monthly instalments. Clients authorize the debit online, and the signed agreement is kept on their file.

  3. 03

    Every payment tracked

    See each payment request and where it stands: sent, opened, paid, failed or refunded. Filter by client, producer or due date, and catch failed payments before they turn into cancellations.

  4. 04

    Posted to Tavio Ledger for you

    Each payment is matched to its invoice and posted to Tavio Ledger, coded to trust or operating according to your setup. The client gets a receipt by email, and the payment appears on their timeline.

  5. 05

    Failed payments, followed up

    When a card is declined or a debit is returned, Tavio creates a task for the CSR and can start a Tavio Remind sequence with a fresh payment link, well before the policy is at risk.

How it works

How Tavio Pay worksHow Tavio Pay works

  1. 1

    Request

    Send a payment link from an invoice, a renewal or a Tavio Remind sequence, by email, SMS or the client portal.

  2. 2

    Pay

    The client pays by card or pre-authorized debit on a secure page in your brokerage's name.

  3. 3

    Match and post

    The payment is matched to its invoice, posted to Tavio Ledger and recorded on the client timeline, and a receipt goes out automatically.

FAQ

Questions, answered.Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Talk to our team.

Which payment methods can clients use?

Tavio Pay is designed for credit card payments and pre-authorized debit, including monthly instalments, on broker-billed invoices and fees. Policies billed directly by the insurer continue to be paid to the insurer. The exact methods available to your brokerage are confirmed during onboarding.

Do client payments go to trust or operating?

You decide in your setup. Payments are coded to trust or operating according to your rules and posted to Tavio Ledger, so your trust records stay accurate. Trust requirements vary by province, so confirm your setup with your accountant.

Can clients pay through the client portal?

Yes. Clients signed in to the Tavio client portal can see what they owe and pay from there, as well as from links in email and SMS.

Does Tavio Pay work alongside premium financing?

Where your brokerage works with a premium finance company, financed policies can be recorded alongside direct payments, so every balance is visible in one place. Premium finance providers are confirmed during onboarding.
