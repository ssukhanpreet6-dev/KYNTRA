Tavio Intelligence

AI that works the file with you.

Tavio Intelligence is the AI built into every Tavio module. It reads documents, flags renewals at risk, drafts correspondence and answers questions about the file. Your team approves what it suggests.

Book a demoSee all products

Tavio is rolling out module by module. Your proposal confirms which modules are live for your go-live date. The AI features that suit your brokerage are agreed during onboarding.

The challenge

Brokers spend their days reading and retyping.Brokers spend their days reading and retyping.

A renewal means reading two policies side by side. A new client means keying a declarations page into the system. Every call means a note someone writes up afterwards. Skilled people end up doing clerical work, and the advice conversation gets squeezed.

  1. 01

    Documents read by hand

    Policies, declarations pages and quotes are read line by line and rekeyed one field at a time.

  2. 02

    Risks spotted too late

    A renewal that is about to leave often shows the signs weeks early, but nobody has time to look across the whole book.

  3. 03

    Every note from scratch

    Letters, follow-ups and call notes start with a blank page, even when the facts are already on the file.

Capabilities

One AI layer, across the whole platform.One AI layer, across the whole platform.

Each capability works inside the module where you need it, with the client file as its source.

  1. 01

    Ask Tavio

    Ask questions about a client, a policy or your book in plain language. Answers come from your brokerage's records, with links to the sources.

  2. 02

    Document reading

    Upload a policy, declarations page or quote and Tavio extracts the insured, coverages, limits, deductibles and dates into the right fields for review.

  3. 03

    Policy comparison

    Compare an expiring policy with its renewal, or several quotes with each other, and see differences in coverage, limits, deductibles and wording highlighted.

  4. 04

    Renewal risk scoring

    Each upcoming renewal gets a risk score built from signals such as premium change, claims, service history and time since last contact, with the reasons shown.

  5. 05

    Email and letter drafting

    Draft renewal letters, coverage explanations, follow-ups and replies that use the facts on the file and your brokerage's tone of voice.

  6. 06

    Summaries and next best actions

    Turn recorded calls and meeting notes into short summaries with follow-ups, and get suggested next steps, such as a coverage gap worth raising before renewal.

    • Call and meeting summaries on the activity timeline
    • Suggested follow-ups you accept, edit or dismiss
    • Client file summaries when you open an account

How it works

  1. 1

    You decide what's switched on

    Administrators choose which AI features are available and to which roles, module by module.

  2. 2

    Grounded in your records

    Tavio Intelligence works from the client file, documents and history in Tavio, within the permissions of the person using it.

  3. 3

    Extractions, drafts, scores and suggestions are presented for review. Nothing is sent, saved or changed until a person approves it.

  4. 4

    Every AI action is logged with who used it, the sources it drew on and what the person did with the result.

FAQ

Questions, answered.Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Talk to our team.

Does Tavio Intelligence make decisions on its own?

No. It reads, compares, drafts and suggests, and people decide. Drafts are never sent automatically, extracted data is reviewed before it's saved, and suggested actions wait for someone to accept them.

How is client data protected when AI is used?

Tavio Intelligence works within the platform's existing protections, including role-based access, encryption and audit logging, and it reads only what the person asking is allowed to see. Our AI approach page and the Trust Center explain how data is handled in more detail.

How accurate is document reading?

Accuracy depends on the quality and layout of each document, so every extracted field is shown next to its source and uncertain values are flagged. A person reviews and confirms the data before it's saved.

Are calls recorded automatically?

No. Call summaries work only on calls your brokerage chooses to record through its telephony integration. Your brokerage controls which calls are recorded and how callers are told.

Can we switch individual AI features off?

Yes. Administrators choose which AI features are available and to which roles, so you can introduce them at your own pace.
