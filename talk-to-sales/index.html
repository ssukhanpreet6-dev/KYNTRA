FAQ

Sales questions.Sales questions.

Do you publish prices?

Not yet. Pricing depends on your brokerage's size and the modules you choose, so we set it out in a written proposal. Our pricing approach page explains how plans work.

Can we talk before booking a demo?

Yes. Many brokerages start with a short call about timing, migration or security, then decide whether a demo is worth the team's time.

Do you work with brokerages outside British Columbia?

Yes. Tavio is built for brokerages across Canada. Insurer connectivity and the availability of specific lines are confirmed during onboarding.

We're an agency in the United States. Can we talk?

Tavio isn't offered in the United States today. Our Coming to the USA page explains our plans, and you can join the waitlist there.
