Regions · Across Canada

From St. John's to Whitehorse.

Tavio is built for Canadian property and casualty brokerages wherever they operate. The platform is designed with national rules such as PIPEDA and CASL in mind, uses CSIO standards for insurer connectivity and respects every time zone, while each province and territory brings its own regulator and requirements.

One country, many rulebooks.One country, many rulebooks.

Every province and territory has its own insurance regulator or licensing body, and the rules for licensing, conduct and client money differ from one to the next. A brokerage licensed in several provinces has to track each set of requirements for each person.

Privacy law follows a similar pattern. PIPEDA generally applies to private-sector organizations in provinces without their own substantially similar law, and to personal information that crosses provincial or national borders. British Columbia, Alberta and Quebec have their own private-sector privacy laws. CASL applies to commercial electronic messages across the country.

Auto insurance differs too. In British Columbia, Saskatchewan and Manitoba, basic auto insurance is provided by a public insurer. Quebec combines a public plan for bodily injury with private insurance for property damage. Elsewhere, auto insurance is written by private insurers.

Quebec and French-language service.Quebec and French-language service.

In Quebec, the Autorité des marchés financiers oversees the distribution of insurance products. Quebec's Law 25 modernized the province's private-sector privacy law, with obligations such as designating a person responsible for the protection of personal information, reporting confidentiality incidents and assessing privacy impacts before personal information is communicated outside Quebec. The Charter of the French language also sets requirements for doing business in French.

FAQ

Questions from brokerages across Canada.Questions from brokerages across Canada.

Do you work with brokerages outside BC, Alberta and Ontario?

Yes. Tavio is designed for property and casualty brokerages anywhere in Canada. Insurer connectivity and the availability of specific lines are confirmed during onboarding.

Is Tavio available in French?

Our French-language site is a placeholder today. If you need French-language service for your team or your clients, tell us what you need and we'll be clear about what Tavio supports now.

Can Tavio support a brokerage licensed in several provinces?

Yes. Licences are recorded by province for each person, branches can keep their own trust accounts, and reports can be viewed by branch or across the whole brokerage.

How do reminders handle different time zones?

Each message waits until your quiet hours end in the client's own time zone, so clients in Halifax and Vancouver each hear from you at a reasonable hour.
