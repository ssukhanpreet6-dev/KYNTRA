Regions · United States

Canada first. The USA next.

Tavio is built for Canadian brokerages today. We plan to bring the platform to independent agencies in the United States, and we'd like to hear from agency leaders who want a say in how it takes shape. Join the waitlist and we'll keep you informed.

Join the waitlistSee the platform

Why we're starting in Canada.Why we're starting in Canada.

Brokerage software only works when it fits the details: the data standards insurers use, the rules regulators set and the way teams actually run their day. We chose to build deeply for Canadian brokerages first, and to do that well before taking on a second market.

The United States brings its own standards, forms, licensing and regulation, which differ from state to state. Bringing Tavio there means building for those properly, with agencies involved from the start.

What we expect to carry over.What we expect to carry over.

The principles behind Tavio stay the same on either side of the border.

  • One system for the whole agency

    Clients, policies, renewals, accounting and reporting on one platform, with every module reading from the same client file.

    Learn more

  • AI with people in control

    AI that reads, drafts and suggests, with results reviewed by a person and every request recorded in an audit trail.

    Learn more

  • Renewals that start themselves

    Renewal pipelines with approval queues, so routine work keeps moving and important files get attention.

    Learn more

  • Security from the start

    Encryption, role-based access and a full audit trail, as set out in our Trust Center.

    Learn more
