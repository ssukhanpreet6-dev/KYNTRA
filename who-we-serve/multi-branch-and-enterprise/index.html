Scale exposes every inconsistency.Scale exposes every inconsistency.

Larger brokerages are often a federation of offices that joined at different times. Branches run renewals on their own calendars, keep their own spreadsheets and report numbers in their own way. Head office stitches it together at month-end, and every report arrives with footnotes.

The stakes rise with size. Licensing has to be tracked across provinces. Access to client information has to match each person's role. Security and privacy reviews ask harder questions, and any new system has to fit an existing identity provider, data warehouse and set of enterprise tools.

FAQ

Questions from multi-branch and enterprise brokerages.Questions from multi-branch and enterprise brokerages.

Can different branches work differently?

Where you choose, yes. Workflows, templates and approval rules can be standard across the brokerage or vary by line of business, and permissions can be set by branch and role.

Can access be restricted by branch?

Yes. Role-based access controls what each person can see and do, so a CSR might see their own branch's clients while regional leadership sees several branches. Every permission change is recorded.

How does Tavio fit with our existing systems?

Through exports to accounting and BI tools, with the Tavio API, webhooks and single sign-on in development. The availability of each integration is confirmed during onboarding. See Integrations and partners for the full catalogue.

Can our security team review Tavio before we commit?

Yes. The Trust Center sets out how Tavio approaches hosting, encryption, access control and privacy, and we'll answer your security team's questions as part of the evaluation.

We operate in several provinces. Does that work?

Yes. Tavio is built for brokerages across Canada. Licences are recorded by province, reminders respect each client's time zone, and branches in different provinces can keep their own trust accounts.
