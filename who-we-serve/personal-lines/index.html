Who we serve · Personal lines

Personal lines volume, without the overtime.

Thousands of home, tenant and condo policies, each with its own renewal, changes and questions. Tavio handles the routine at scale, from renewals that open themselves to documents clients download on their own, so your team has time for advice.

High volume. Thin margins. Rising expectations.High volume. Thin margins. Rising expectations.

Personal lines is a volume business. Each policy carries a modest commission, and the same few tasks repeat thousands of times a year: a mortgagee added, an address changed, a declarations page resent, a renewal explained. When premiums rise, more clients shop around, and every renewal conversation takes longer.

Clients compare your service with the apps they use for everything else, and direct writers are a click away. What a brokerage offers is advice and a choice of markets. Both take time, and time is exactly what routine work consumes.

FAQ

Questions from personal lines brokerages.Questions from personal lines brokerages.

Does Tavio quote personal auto?

Not today. Tavio is built for home, property and commercial lines, so Tavio Quote compares home, tenant and condo results side by side. Supported lines and insurer availability are confirmed during onboarding.

How does Tavio work in provinces with public auto insurance?

Basic auto transactions are completed in the public insurer's own systems, and Tavio does not handle personal auto policies today. Coming soon, Tavio Forms will prefill vehicle transfer and tax forms for your team to review. Talk to us about the counter workflows you run.

Can clients get their documents without calling?

Yes. Tavio Portal lets clients download the declarations pages, receipts and lender letters your team shares, at any hour, and each download is recorded on the file. The documents available depend on what each insurer issues.

What about vehicle transfer and tax forms?

Tavio Forms, coming soon, is being built to prefill vehicle transfer and tax forms, along with applications and change requests, from the client file and the documents clients bring in, such as a registration or bill of sale. Your team reviews each form, VIN included, before it is signed.

How do you help with rising rebuilding costs?

Tavio Valuation recalculates replacement-cost estimates before renewal and flags homes where the dwelling limit falls short. Tavio Intelligence can draft a plain-language letter explaining the options for your team to review.
